the reading

The volumes cut the table by article and run the places down it. These blocks cut the other way — one place, its goods under it — so every figure in a block is that place's own figure, and the sum at the foot is what that place sent, not what the article came to across the world.

The great colonial goods — Zucker, Caffee, Sago, Reis, Taback, Cacao, Baumwolle — are weighed net, said once at the head of the printed block and carried down it on ditto marks. Read line by line those marks are lost, so they are restored here as ℔ Nto. Everything else keeps the measure the page gave it.
