our stock is dark and firing the laser bleaches it pale, so the only thing that can come out light is what the head actually burns. to get a picture that reads as itself — not as a negative — the head has to fire on its light tones: the bright parts become pale scar, the dark parts are simply left as bare brick. that is the default, and it is set under laser. fire on the dark tones instead only for black-on-white artwork you want burnt as drawn.
